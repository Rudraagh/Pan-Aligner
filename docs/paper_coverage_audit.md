Paper Coverage Audit
====================
Paper checked: Rajput et al. (2024), 'Co-linear chaining on pangenome graphs'

Implemented or reasonably reproduced in this project
- Real minigraph graph construction and real PanAligner execution are wired into the pipeline.
- GAF parsing is implemented, including identity, coverage, mapping quality, normalized score, traversed nodes, and best-alignment selection. Coverage is the aligned query interval divided by query length (it cannot exceed 1).
- Held-out evaluation is implemented for the local gene datasets and writes alignment_metrics.json, alignment_records.json, and evaluation_report.txt.
- Dataset-specific paper-style local artifacts are generated: local graph property tables, mapping-quality cutoff sweep CSV/PNG outputs, and a local reproduction report.
- Truth-labelled correctness at locus scale, using the paper's own method (PanAligner/data/simulated_reads.sh and evaluate.sh): PBSIM2 reads with embedded true intervals, `paftools.js mapeval` scoring, PanAligner v1.0 and minigraph 0.21 on the combined graphs and the linear reference, reads from the reference and from the held-out haplotypes, three seeds, and Fig. 6-style MAPQ curves (`scripts/simulate_truth_reads.py`, `scripts/truth_eval.py`, `outputs/truth_eval/REPORT.md`).
- Graph statistics and PNG visualizations are implemented for built graphs and example alignments.
- Educational reproductions exist for SCCs, DFS back-edge removal, DAG approximation, path cover, anchors, precedence, chaining, gap-cost analysis, and convergence plots.

Partially reproduced
- The paper's algorithmic ideas are demonstrated, but several graph-theory stages are implemented as simplified educational demos rather than the exact PanAligner preprocessing pipeline from the paper.
- Correctness evaluation follows the paper's method but at locus scale (APP/PSEN1/PSEN2 regions of 57-290 kb), not on the whole-genome 10H/40H/80H/95H graphs. The held-out haplotypes differ from the reference by one or two small variants, so PanAligner and minigraph are effectively tied; this benchmark is too easy to separate them. The strict mapeval errors are paths through non-reference nodes; after projecting those paths onto the reference, one read is misplaced (a 415 bp partial minigraph alignment, PSEN1, held-out reads).
- A local approximation of the hybrid Minigraph-PanAligner idea is implemented for this dataset, but it is not the paper's full benchmarked hybrid workflow.

Missing compared with the paper's reported results and outputs
- Table 1 graph-scale benchmark properties for 10H, 40H, 80H, and 95H are not generated by this project; the whole-genome inputs are not available here.
- Table 2 path-cover ranges, anchor-count statistics, and iteration counts for last2reach, D, and chaining are not computed on the real graphs here.
- Figure 5 lower-bound vs computed-path-cover comparison is not implemented for the real graphs.
- Tables 3 and 4 runtime, memory, unaligned-read, and incorrectly-aligned-read comparisons versus Minigraph and GraphAligner are not implemented at paper scale; GraphAligner is not included in the truth evaluation. Recorded wall times are single laptop/WSL calls, not runtime benchmarks.
- The exact Fig. 6 whole-genome comparison against multiple aligners is not reproduced; only the locus-scale PanAligner/minigraph curves above exist.
- Table 5 DAG benchmarking versus Minichain, GraphChainer, Minigraph, and GraphAligner is not implemented.

Separate from the paper reproduction
- The proposed alignment-free methodology (`scripts/proposed/`, `python main.py --proposed ...`) and its structural quality scores are the project's own prototype, not part of the paper.
- The CUDA minimizer path has exact CPU parity and a minimizer-only speed-up on one laptop GPU (`outputs/gpu_validation/laptop/`); it is not part of the paper and does not affect PanAligner.
- The shared held-out-query comparison (`scripts/shared_query_eval.py`) is an alignment-free representation check, not a paper benchmark.

Bottom line
This repo reproduces the paper at a workflow-and-concepts level, plus a locus-scale version of the paper's truth-labelled correctness evaluation for PanAligner and minigraph. It does not reproduce the paper's whole-genome benchmark tables and figures, GraphAligner comparisons, or runtime/memory results. It is accurate to present it as a partial practical reproduction plus a theory/demo layer, not as a complete reimplementation of every experiment, table, and figure in the paper.
